Weekly Budget Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

What the app does

Weekly Budget is a personal budgeting web app. You enter bills, income, savings and paystub amounts, and it shows weekly and yearly totals.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter is stored only in your own browser on your own device. The app has no server and no account system. The developer cannot see your data.

Paystubs

When you choose a paystub file, it is read on your device to fill in amounts. The file is not uploaded and is not saved.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to use Google Drive backup, the app asks for permission to create and read files that it creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It saves one file named weekly-budget-backup.json containing your budget entries. It cannot see any other file in your Drive. The file goes only to your own Google Drive account and is not sent anywhere else. You can delete it from Drive at any time, and you can remove the app's access in your Google Account settings.

What we do not do

The app does not collect personal information, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not share or sell data.
